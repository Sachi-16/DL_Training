TITLE: STUDENT TRAINING, INTERNSHIP REGULATIONS AND PLACEMENT POLICY MANUAL
OPERATIONAL CYCLE: 2024-2025

1. PLACEMENT REGISTRATION AND ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
1.1 Minimum Academic Qualification for Campus Registration
To register with the Training & Placement Cell (TPO) for the final campus placement season, a candidate must satisfy all of the following conditions:
- Completion of all registered courses up to the end of the 6th semester.
- Minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 6.75 across semesters 1 through 6. (Note: Specific hiring companies may enforce higher cutoffs, typically 7.00, 7.50, or 8.00 CGPA).
- A candidate must have no more than one (1) active academic backlog at the time of company registration. All standing backlogs must be declared truthfully on the placement registration portal.

1.2 Placement Registration Fee and Mandatory Training
A non-refundable placement facilitation fee of INR 2,000 must be paid prior to July 31. Registered students must undergo a compulsory 60-hour Soft Skills and Technical Aptitude Bootcamp during the pre-final semester break. Students having attendance below 85% in the bootcamp will be barred from Day-1 and Day-2 placement drive registrations.

2. RECRUITMENT CLASSIFICATION AND ONE-STUDENT-ONE-JOB POLICY
2.1 Tiered Company Structure
Campus recruiters are classified into three official compensation tiers based on Annual Cost-to-Company (CTC):
- Tier 3 (Core & Mass Recruiters): Annual CTC up to INR 6.50 LPA.
- Tier 2 (Standard Professional Recruiters): Annual CTC between INR 6.51 LPA and INR 11.99 LPA.
- Tier 1 (Dream Companies / Marquee Offers): Annual CTC of INR 12.00 LPA or higher.

2.2 The One-Student-One-Job Protocol
To ensure equitable placement opportunities across the graduating student body, the institute operates under a strict One-Student-One-Job philosophy:
- When a candidate receives a formal job offer from a company in Tier 3 or Tier 2, they are declared "Placed" and are automatically removed from participation in further Tier 3 and Tier 2 recruitment drives.
- Dream Company Exception: A placed candidate is permitted to sit for recruitment in Tier 1 ("Dream") companies provided the Dream offer CTC is at least 1.5 times higher than the student's existing confirmed offer.
- A student who secures an offer in a Dream Company is strictly locked from all subsequent campus drives. Under no circumstances may a student hold more than two institutional campus offers.

3. SUMMER INTERNSHIPS AND INDUSTRY PROJECTS
3.1 Mandatory Internship Requirement
Every student must complete a structured industry summer internship lasting between 8 and 10 weeks following the 6th semester. The internship carries 3 academic credits upon presentation of the internship completion certificate and oral viva defense before a departmental committee.

3.2 No Objection Certificate (NOC) for Term-Time Internships
Students selected for full-semester off-campus 8th-semester industrial internships must obtain a formal No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the TPO and the Head of Department (HOD). An NOC is issued only if the candidate has secured a minimum CGPA of 7.50 with zero backlogs.

4. STUDENT CODE OF CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINARY SANCTIONS
4.1 Interview Absenteeism and Walkouts
If a student registers for a recruitment drive, gets shortlisted in the online test or resume screening, and subsequently fails to report for the physical/virtual personal interview without prior authorized medical leave, they will face:
- Immediate debarment from the next three (3) consecutive campus recruitment drives.
- Blacklisting from all Tier 1 (Dream) company applications for the entire placement year on a second violation.

4.2 Academic Duty Leave (DL) for Placement Activities
Students participating in official on-campus or off-campus aptitude tests, group discussions, and recruitment interviews organized by the TPO will receive Academic Duty Leave (DL) for scheduled classes. A maximum of 15 days of Duty Leave can be claimed per student in their final academic year.
