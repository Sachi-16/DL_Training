TITLE: INSTITUTIONAL SCHOLARSHIP SCHEMES, FINANCIAL AID AND FEE WAIVERS
REVISED: JANUARY 2024

1. MERIT SCHOLARSHIP SCHEMES
1.1 Chairman's Super-Merit Fellowship (Category A)
- Eligibility: Granted to the top 2% academic rank-holders in each academic branch based on the annual Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA). Minimum CGPA required is 9.50 with zero lifetime course failures or backlogs.
- Benefit: 100% waiver of annual academic tuition fees (excludes hostel and mess charges).
- Renewal Condition: Must maintain CGPA of 9.25 or higher each academic year.

1.2 Dean's Merit Scholarship (Category B)
- Eligibility: Granted to students falling between the top 2.1% and top 5% of their branch cohort. Minimum CGPA required is 8.80 with zero active backlogs.
- Benefit: 50% waiver of annual academic tuition fees.
- Renewal Condition: Must maintain CGPA of 8.50 or higher and 75% attendance across all subjects.

2. NEED-BASED FINANCIAL AID (EWS PROGRAM)
2.1 Economically Weaker Section (EWS) Fee Assistance
- Objective: To support meritorious students from economically challenged backgrounds.
- Eligibility Criteria: Total gross annual family income from all sources must not exceed INR 3,50,000 (Rupees Three Lakh Fifty Thousand only). Student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.00 with no active backlogs.
- Documentation: Valid Income Certificate issued by an official not below the rank of Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) or Tahsildar, accompanied by previous year IT Returns or BPL card copy. Annual application window opens July 1 and closes August 31.
- Benefit: 75% subsidy on tuition fees for the full academic session.

3. SPORTS AND CO-CURRICULAR EXCELLENCE GRANTS
3.1 National Sports Fellowship
- Eligibility: Students who represent Apex Institute or the State/Country in recognized Association of Indian Universities (AIU) National Games, Khelo India University Games, or Olympic sporting disciplines.
- Academic Maintenance: The recipient must maintain at least 65% academic attendance (utilizing Sports Duty Leaves) and a minimum CGPA of 6.50.
- Benefit: 50% waiver on tuition fees and free access to campus gymnasium and sports training facilities.

4. TUITION FEE REFUND AND ADMISSION CANCELLATION RULES
4.1 Withdrawal Timelines and Refund Schedule
In accordance with statutory guidelines, if a newly admitted student formally applies for admission cancellation through the Registrar's portal, refunds are disbursed as follows:
- Notice of withdrawal received 15 or more calendar days before the formally notified commencement of classes: 100% refund of tuition and academic fees, minus an administrative processing fee of INR 1,000.
- Notice received less than 15 calendar days before class commencement: 90% refund of aggregate tuition fees.
- Notice received within 15 calendar days after the class commencement date: 80% refund of aggregate tuition fees.
- Notice received between 16 and 30 calendar days after class commencement: 50% refund of aggregate tuition fees.
- Notice received beyond 30 calendar days after class commencement: No tuition fee refund is admissible. Only the Institute Security / Caution Deposit (INR 10,000) will be refunded in full.

5. SPECIAL PROVISIONS AND DUAL-BENEFIT EXCLUSION
5.1 Concurrency Rule
No student is entitled to claim or receive two simultaneous institutional fee waiver scholarships. If a student qualifies for both a Merit Scholarship and an EWS Financial Aid grant, they must choose one or will automatically be granted the scheme offering the higher monetary relief. External government scholarships (such as National Scholarship Portal schemes) may be availed concurrently if external rules permit.
