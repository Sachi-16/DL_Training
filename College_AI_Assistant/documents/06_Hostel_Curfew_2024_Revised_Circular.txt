APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
DIRECTORATE OF STUDENT WELFARE & RESIDENTIAL AFFAIRS
CIRCULAR CODE: DSW-CIRC-2024-09
TITLE: EXECUTIVE AMENDMENT TO RESIDENTIAL HOSTEL TIMINGS AND VISITOR PROTOCOLS
DATE OF ISSUE: AUGUST 1, 2024
TARGET AUDIENCE: ALL RESIDENT STUDENTS, WARDENS, AND CAMPUS SECURITY

1. CONTEXT AND REASON FOR AMENDMENT
In response to student representations submitted via the Student Council and following review by the Residential Advisory Board on July 20, 2024, the competent authority has approved key revisions to the residential code of conduct previously established under Document RES-RULE-2023.

2. REVISED HOSTEL CURFEW HOURS (SUPERSEDES RES-RULE-2023 SECTION 2.1)
Effective from the fall semester starting August 1, 2024:
- The mandatory daily hostel gate curfew for all undergraduate residential blocks is hereby extended from 9:00 PM to 10:00 PM on weekdays (Sunday through Thursday).
- Weekend Curfew Relaxation: On Friday and Saturday evenings, the hostel turnstiles will remain open until 10:30 PM.
- Biometric check-in turnstiles will officially lock at 10:00 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends. All resident students must enter their respective hostel blocks before these revised cutoff hours.

3. REVISED LATE ENTRY PENALTY STRUCTURE (SUPERSEDES RES-RULE-2023 SECTION 2.2)
The disciplinary penalty for late entry is revised to ease the financial burden on students while maintaining security discipline:
- Arrivals between 10:01 PM and 10:30 PM on weekdays will be logged as 'Late Entry - Tier 1' and subject to a reduced administrative penalty fee of INR 250 (lowered from the previous INR 500 charge).
- Arrivals after 10:30 PM require written verification from the resident warden and counseling before the DSW.

4. EXTENDED VISITOR LOUNGE ACCESS HOURS (SUPERSEDES RES-RULE-2023 SECTION 4.1)
Day-scholars, parents, and authorized visitors are now permitted access to the ground-floor hostel reception and visitor lounge areas until 8:30 PM daily (extended from the earlier 7:00 PM restriction). Residential wings and upper floors remain strictly out-of-bounds for non-residents at all times.

5. CONTINUATION OF SAFETY CLAUSES
All other provisions of RES-RULE-2023, including the ban on high-power electrical appliances exceeding 500W, overnight guest restrictions, and quiet hours from 10:30 PM to 6:00 AM, remain in full force.
