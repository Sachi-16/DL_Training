TITLE: HOSTEL CODE OF CONDUCT, LIVING REGULATIONS AND DISCIPLINARY MANUAL
ORIGINAL ADOPTION: JULY 2023

1. HOSTEL ADMISSION AND ROOM ALLOTMENT
1.1 Room Allocation
Hostel accommodation is allocated on an annual double-occupancy or triple-occupancy basis based on semester CGPA. Room allotment cannot be traded, sub-let, or exchanged without written permission from the Chief Warden.

1.2 Room Check-in and Inventory
At the commencement of each academic year, residents must sign the room inventory sheet verifying furniture, light fixtures, ceiling fans, and door keys. Any damage discovered during end-of-year checkout will be deducted from the student's residential security deposit.

2. ENTRY TIMINGS, GATE PASSES AND NIGHT CURFEW
2.1 Residential Curfew (Baseline 2023 Rule)
The main gates of all undergraduate hostel blocks (Boys Hostels H1-H4 and Girls Hostels H5-H7) close strictly at 9:00 PM daily. All resident students must complete biometric fingerprint or face recognition check-in at the security turnstiles prior to 9:00 PM.

2.2 Late Entry Penalties
- Any student arriving at the hostel gate between 9:01 PM and 9:30 PM will be marked 'Late Entry - Tier 1' and charged an administrative penalty of INR 500 on the college ERP portal.
- Arrival past 9:30 PM is categorized as an 'Unauthorized Absence / Tier 2 Violation', incurring a penalty of INR 1,000, mandatory written explanation to the Warden, and automated SMS notification to registered parents or local guardians.
- Three late entries in a single calendar month result in immediate suspension of hostel gate pass privileges for four weeks.

2.3 Night-Out and Weekend Leave Policy
Residents intending to stay out overnight or leave campus for home visits must submit an electronic Gate Pass request through the Student Portal at least 24 hours prior to departure. A gate pass exceeding two nights requires an automated One-Time Password (OTP) validation from the parent's registered mobile number before gate security clears the pass.

3. ELECTRICAL APPLIANCES AND ROOM SAFETY
3.1 Prohibited Electrical Devices
To avoid electrical circuit overloads and fire hazards, heating appliances exceeding 500 Watts are strictly forbidden inside hostel rooms. Prohibited items include:
- Electric water immersion rods
- Electric room heaters and blowers
- Induction cooktops, microwave ovens, and electric hot plates
- Heavy clothes pressing irons (residents must use the laundry room)
- Air conditioners and personal desert coolers (unless medical clearance is granted)

3.2 Appliance Confiscation and Fines
Any prohibited appliance detected during random quarterly warden inspections will be confiscated until the end of the academic year. A monetary fine of INR 1,500 will be levied per prohibited appliance detected.

4. GUEST REGULATIONS AND VISITOR PROTOCOL
4.1 Day Visitors and Day Scholars
Visitors, parents, guardians, and non-resident day-scholar students are permitted to visit residents only in designated ground-floor Visitor Lounges between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM on weekdays, and 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM on Sundays. Non-residents are strictly prohibited from entering residential wings, hallways, or student rooms past 7:00 PM.

4.2 Overnight Guests
Hostel rooms cannot accommodate outside guests overnight under any circumstances. Unauthorized accommodation of an external person overnight attracts an immediate disciplinary fine of INR 2,500 on the room resident and referral to the Proctorial Board for potential hostel eviction.

5. QUIET HOURS AND COMMUNITY STANDARDS
Quiet hours must be observed in all residential corridors and study halls from 10:30 PM to 6:00 AM daily. Playing loud acoustic speakers, shouting, or disruptive musical rehearsals during quiet hours is prohibited.
